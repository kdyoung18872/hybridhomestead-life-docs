# Privacy Policy for HybridHomestead.Life

## 1. Introduction

HybridHomestead.Life ("App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Authentication
The App uses OAuth 2.0 authentication with Microsoft Entra ID. When you log in, we collect:
- Your account email
- Basic profile information (name, photo if provided)
- OAuth authentication tokens (refresh tokens stored securely)

### User Data
Information you provide directly:
- Dietary restrictions and allergies
- Recipes you favorite or save
- Garden selections (plants you grow)
- Meal plans you create
- Grocery lists you export

### Usage Data
We may collect:
- Features you use
- Recipes you view or customize
- Crash reports (to improve stability)
- Performance metrics

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Authenticate your account securely
- Provide app features (recipes, meal planning, etc.)
- Personalize your experience (dietary profile, garden)
- Improve app stability and performance
- Comply with legal requirements

## 4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is:
- Stored securely on your device (device-first architecture)
- Encrypted in transit using HTTPS
- Protected by OAuth 2.0 authentication
- Never shared with third parties

Authentication tokens are stored in secure device storage (iOS Keychain). We follow industry best practices for security.

## 5. Your Rights

You can:
- Request access to your data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Export your data (meal plans, favorites)
- Change your privacy settings anytime

## 6. Third-Party Services

This app uses:
- Microsoft Entra ID for authentication
- Microsoft Privacy Policy: https://privacy.microsoft.com/

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We'll notify you of significant changes.

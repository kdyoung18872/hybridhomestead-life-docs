# HybridHomestead.Life Support
## Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is HybridHomestead.Life?
A: HybridHomestead.Life is an app that connects sustainable gardening with allergen-friendly cooking. Grow what you eat, eat what you grow!
Q: Do I need an account?
A: Yes, you sign in with OAuth authentication using Microsoft Entra ID. No passwords to remember—just use your existing account.
Q: How is my data protected?
A: We use industry-standard OAuth 2.0 authentication and all your data is stored securely on your device. We never share personal information with third parties.
Q: How do I customize a recipe to my allergies?
A: Create your dietary profile with your allergies and restrictions. When you view a recipe, use the AI customization feature to adapt it to your needs while preserving the creator's style.
Q: Can I export my grocery list?
A: Yes! Generate a grocery list from your meal plan and export it to share or copy to your phone's notes app.
Q: What if a recipe has an ingredient I can't use?
A: Use the AI customization feature to swap ingredients. The app will suggest alternatives that maintain the recipe's integrity.
Q: Is this only for experienced gardeners?
A: No! Our planting guides are designed for beginners. Each guide includes step-by-step instructions and seasonal calendars.
Q: Do I need to be a "hybrid homesteader"?
A: Not at all. Whether you grow nothing, a few herbs, or a full garden—the app adapts to your level. Perfect for anyone interested in healthier, sustainable eating.
Q: What's a "hybrid homesteader"?
A: Our term for people who balance modern life with homesteading. You don't need to be off-grid—just interested in growing some of your own food.
Q: How many recipes are included?
A: We launch with 44+ allergen-friendly recipes, with new recipes added regularly.
Q: Are all recipes allergen-free?
A: All recipes are free from major allergens (gluten, dairy, soy, seed oil). You can further customize each recipe to your specific dietary needs.
Q: Can I suggest new recipes?
A: We'd love feedback! Contact us at support@hybridhomestead.life with recipe suggestions.
Q: Is there a web version?
A: Currently available on iOS. Android and web versions planned for future releases.
Q: Can I use the app offline?
A: Most features work offline (recipes, meal planning, grocery lists). Account login requires internet.
Q: I found a bug. How do I report it?
A: Please email us at support@hybridhomestead.life with:
Description of the problem
Steps to reproduce
Your device and iOS version
Screenshots if possible
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes! The free version includes the full recipe library and basic meal planning. Premium features available with subscription.
Q: What's the difference between free and premium?
A:
Free: Recipe library, basic meal planning, planting guides
Premium: Advanced meal planning, garden tracking, exclusive recipes, priority support
